01 · About

Two kinds of discipline

I'm Vidhaath Pillai, a first-year B.Tech Computer Science & AI student at NST-RU who is drawn to discipline in two forms: the physical kind built through training, and the technical kind built at a terminal.

On one side, I train in Shotokan karate, where I hold a brown belt, and I swim regularly. Both teach focus, endurance and staying calm under pressure. On the other, I'm learning Python and Linux, the foundations for understanding how systems work and how they can be protected.

Both point to the same goal. I want to become a soldier in the Indian Army and, in time, use cybersecurity skills to help the Indian Armed Forces. Reading keeps me curious along the way.
